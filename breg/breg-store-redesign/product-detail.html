Product Description

The Breg T Scope Post-Op Knee Brace is designed to provide superior stabilization and support during post-operative recovery and rehabilitation. Engineered with precision and comfort in mind, this brace features adjustable ROM (Range of Motion) hinges that allow healthcare providers to customize the level of mobility based on the patient's recovery stage.

Ideal for ACL/PCL/MCL/LCL reconstruction, meniscus repair, and other knee surgeries, this brace combines durability with patient comfort. The lightweight aluminum frame ensures structural integrity while minimizing patient burden during extended wear periods.

Key Features

Adjustable ROM hinges (0° to 120° flexion/extension)
Bilateral design fits left or right knee
Latex-free, hypoallergenic materials
Breathable foam padding for patient comfort
Tool-free hinge adjustments for quick modifications
Reinforced strapping system for secure fit
Radiolucent frame allows for X-rays without removal
Machine washable liner for easy maintenance

Primary Indications

Post-Operative Care

  • ACL/PCL reconstruction
  • MCL/LCL repair
  • Meniscus repair or meniscectomy
  • Total knee arthroplasty

Conservative Treatment

  • Ligament sprains or tears
  • Patellar instability
  • Post-traumatic knee injuries
  • Chronic knee instability
